1. The short version

  • Local by default. Everything you log — workouts, body weight, height, age, sex, nutrition, and Mind & Spirit entries — is stored on your device. Nothing leaves it unless you create an account.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs and does not fingerprint or track you across apps or websites. Fonts are bundled, so no third-party request is made to render the app.
  • If you sign in, we store your email, a public profile your friends can see, and an encrypted-in-transit backup of your full app data so you can restore it on another device.
  • You can delete everything from Settings → Privacy → Delete account, at any time, without contacting us.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not use health, fitness or religious-practice data for advertising, profiling or any purpose other than showing it back to you.
  • We do not read your device contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos.
  • We do not read data from Apple Health, Health Connect or any other app. If a future version adds that integration, it will ask for permission first and this policy will be updated before release.

4. Service providers

Account, database and backup storage are provided by Supabase, hosted in [region of your Supabase project]. Supabase processes data on our behalf under its data processing terms. The web version of the app is served from GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may log standard web-server data such as IP address for security purposes.

5. Moderation

A small number of administrator accounts can view public profiles, suspend accounts that abuse the social features, and send a standard password-reset email to a user who requests it. Administrators cannot see your password and cannot open your private backup. Administrative actions are logged.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Device data stays until you clear it in the app, uninstall it, or clear the browser's site data.
  • Account data stays until you delete the account. Settings → Privacy → Delete account removes your backup, profile, friendships and sign-in immediately and permanently. Your on-device data is kept unless you clear it.
  • You can export all of your data at any time as a JSON file from Settings → Data backup.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA/CPRA), you have the right to access, correct, export, restrict or delete your personal data, and to object to processing. The export and delete tools above satisfy most requests instantly; for anything else contact [contact email]. We will respond within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

8. Children

Iron Realm is not directed at children under 13 (or the age of digital consent in your country) and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Data in transit is encrypted with TLS. Cloud data is protected by per-user row-level security so accounts can only read and write their own records. Passwords are hashed by our provider and never stored in plain text. No system is perfectly secure; if you discover a vulnerability please report it to [security contact email].

10. Changes

We will update this page and the effective date when practices change. Material changes will be announced in the app before they take effect.
